# Fortune-5 DfCM production closure

AshR2RML production standing is not a slogan, a generated mapping file, or a green unit test. It is a bounded receipt over the exact admitted subject.

The Fortune-5 closure preserves the maximal lawful production candidates while refusing unsupported promotion:

1. **Spark** admits the DSL and semantic metadata.
2. **SemanticIR / Mapping IR** remains the canonical subject identity.
3. **Reactor** orchestrates admitted steps and emits telemetry; it does not become a compiler or an authority surface.
4. **Igniter** installs and generates Ash-native source at development time; it does not render Turtle or mutate runtime production systems.
5. **ggen** receives deterministic path/content projections and performs external manufacturing with its own receipts.
6. **BRCE** is the exclusive DO path. No actuation is admitted without identity, authority, consequence, replay, and receipt.

## Production invariants

The default closure requires:

- P99 cold-path latency at or below 500 ms.
- At least 1,000,000 concurrent operations as the stated stress target.
- horizontal scaling, partitioned execution, and backpressure.
- multi-region and multi-AZ availability with rolling deployment, failover, and disaster-recovery evidence.
- OpenTelemetry-style trace context, semantic receipt hashes, P95/P99 histograms, and decision logs.
- least privilege, no ambient authority, externalized secrets, tenant isolation, signed release, SBOM, and vulnerability gate.
- OTP supervision, fault isolation, deterministic recovery, replay, idempotency, schema validation, SHACL validation, migration dry-run, and rollback plan.

## Standing ladder

- `BLOCKED`: a hard production invariant failed.
- `PARTIAL_ALIVE`: the production contract is admitted, but exact runtime evidence or cutover authority is missing.
- `ALIVE`: the exact subject has hard production checks, runtime observation, stress observation, exact-head CI, OBDA crown receipt, and cutover authority.

Unit, integration, protocol, and CI checks can support the ladder, but none of them may individually crown production readiness unless they are bound to the same admitted subject.

## Falsifiers

The closure is falsified when any hard invariant fails, when selected topology is outside the preserved candidates, when a DO request lacks BRCE identity, or when runtime/cutover evidence is represented as present without exact receipt identity.
